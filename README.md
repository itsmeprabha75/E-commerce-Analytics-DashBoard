# Vivo Calif E-Commerce Analytics Dashboard

A comprehensive e-commerce analytics dashboard providing real-time insights into orders, customer behavior, product performance, and business metrics for Vivo Calif.

## 📊 Overview

This dashboard provides a complete view of e-commerce operations with 2,400 orders totaling $649.0k in revenue, featuring multi-dimensional analysis across various business aspects.

## ✨ Key Features

### Business Metrics
- **Order Tracking**: Monitor 2,400 orders with 11,997 items
- **Revenue Analysis**: Track $649.0k in total sales
- **Performance Metrics**: 4.0 average rating, 2.3 days average delivery time

### Order Channel Analysis
Monitor orders across multiple platforms:
- Mobile App (primary channel)
- Instagram
- Partner App
- Target.com
- Website

### Comprehensive Analytics

#### 1. **Trend Analysis**
- 13-week order and quantity trends
- Visual tracking of business growth patterns
- Dual-axis visualization for orders and quantities

#### 2. **Customer Demographics**
- Gender distribution analysis (Female/Male/Other/Unknown)
- Channel-specific gender preferences
- Detailed percentage breakdowns across all platforms

#### 3. **Product Performance**
Track popularity across 15+ product categories:
- T-Shirts & Jeans (top performers)
- Sneakers & Tank Tops
- Bikinis & Shorts
- Accessories (Jewelry, Tote Bags, etc.)
- Apparel (Sundresses, Graphic Tees, etc.)

#### 4. **Geographical Insights**
- Interactive US map showing order distribution
- Quantity heatmap across states
- Regional performance analysis

#### 5. **Order Quantity Distribution**
- Detailed breakdown from single to bulk orders (10+)
- Peak analysis at orders of 2 and 3 items
- Frequency distribution visualization

#### 6. **Shipment Duration Analysis**
- Delivery time tracking (0-14 days)
- Performance optimization insights
- Distribution of shipment speeds

#### 7. **Customer Satisfaction**
- Monthly satisfaction trends (Jan-Mar)
- Satisfaction vs dissatisfaction tracking
- Temporal analysis of customer sentiment

## 🎯 Use Cases

- **Business Intelligence**: Track overall business performance and KPIs
- **Marketing Optimization**: Identify best-performing channels and demographics
- **Inventory Management**: Understand product demand patterns
- **Logistics Planning**: Optimize delivery times and regional distribution
- **Customer Experience**: Monitor satisfaction and improve service quality
